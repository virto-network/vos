## 1. Zero knowledge

**1a. The blinding fraction joined to the last batch is sound and hiding
for every chip.** `MaskedChipEval::evaluate` (`pvm/proof/src/masked/mod.rs:85-97`)
adds `b / combine_B(1)` after the chip's fractions and finalizes with
`Logup::batches(n, 1)`, which puts it in the last batch. Stwo's batched
constraint for a batch with denominators `d_1…d_k` is
`(ΔS + cs/N)·Πd − Σ n_i Π_{j≠i} d_j` for the last batch and `col·Πd − Σ…`
for the others. Joining `b/1` adds the term `b·Πd`, of degree `1 + deg Πd`,
exactly the degree of the `ΔS·Πd` term already there, so **no constraint's
degree rises**, for `Pairs` (cubic) and `Single` (quadratic) alike; the
masked audit's histogram `[0, 23, 4395, 759]` equalling the plain one is the
expected result, not a coincidence. A batch mixing fractions of different
relations is a sum of fractions whichever relations they read; the blind is
one more summand. Soundness of the blinding itself rests on the order of the
transcript: `b` is committed in the main tree (`commit_main`,
`recair/src/masked/mod.rs:573-610`) before the relations and `BlindRelation`
are drawn (`:500`), so a prover cannot choose `Σ b_c / combine_B(1)` to cancel
a relation imbalance `ε(α, z)` except with probability about `p^-4` per
attempt; the gate `an_unbalanced_relation_cancelled_by_the_blinding_is_refused`
(`recair/tests/segment/gates.rs:247-287`) is the right test and passes.

**1b. The unblinded boundary sums are not a leak.** Each of the four is a
deterministic function of `(initial_state, final_state)` and the drawn
relations, and the verifier recomputes it (`check_boundary_claimed_sums`,
`pvm/proof/src/boundary_binding.rs:146-199`); a value the verifier can
compute from public data reveals nothing beyond that data. The boundary
states themselves are the statement (`public_values`, `masked/mod.rs:311-320`,
mixed after the main root). The pad rows add rows with `is_real = 0`, which
contribute nothing to those sums. The 13 blinded sums with `Σ b = 0` reveal
only `Σ_blinded s_c = −Σ_public s_c`, which is public. The right-guess gate
(`leakage.rs:263-290`) checks exactly this split.

**1c. "Full `h` for every chip" holds.** Every chip's interaction trace ends
in a running sum whose constraint reads the previous row, so every component
has a `|O| = 2` column; `mask_rows` (`recair/src/masked/mod.rs:118-143`)
takes the widest offsets of the component, so each gets the format's `h`.
The per-component `h` is correct and generic but inert on this AIR;
`proving.md` §8's amendment is right. (A per-*column* `h` would save only
R1's matrix width and random draws, not rows: the component's floor is set
by its widest column. See §5.)

**1d. The pads, the lengths and the active set.** The pads leak nothing a
column reveals: table repeats carry zero multiplicity, boundary/code pads
`is_real = 0`, all committed masked. But two shape values are public and, in
7.14, **witness-dependent**: `log_sizes` are `max(natural, floor)`
(`masked_segment_trace`, `masked/trace.rs:46-73`: a run above the floor
publishes `⌈log2 steps⌉` of its CPU, ledgers and tables) and `active` is
whatever the caller passes (the tests pass `active_component_mask(side_note)`,
the chips the *run* touched, `tests/segment/support.rs:71-73`: a run with no
division shows no DIVREM chip). The two leakage kernels share a shape by
construction, so `the_shape_is_the_format_and_the_coins` (`leakage.rs:296-334`)
passes without testing this. This is pin J's "witness-independent trace"
(the program id pins the mask and the sizes; the prover pads to them and
fails above them), scheduled for 7.15, and the record's "one shape for two
kernels" must not be read as a zero-knowledge result. The record should say
so (M3). `prove_segment` takes no target shape today; 7.15 needs a
`Sizes::Pinned`-like argument and a gate that a chip the run never touches
proves at the pinned size with all-padding rows.

Design-wise, the active set is public (part of the program id) and that is
right; it is witness-dependent only until 7.15 pins it.

## 2. Soundness

**2a. Finalize in evaluation is equivalent.** Inspected all 25 chips: every
removed `finalize_logup()` (14, now `Logup::Single`: bitcount,
bitwise_and_byte, bitwise_lookup, byte_to_bits, jump_table, memory,
memory_root_boundary, popcount, power_of_two, program_memory,
range_multiplicity, register_memory, register_memory_boundary,
register_memory_closing) and `finalize_logup_in_pairs()` (11, `Pairs`) was
the last statement of `add_constraints`, and `BuiltInComponentEval::evaluate`
calls `C::LOGUP.finalize` immediately after it (`framework/eval.rs:31`). Same
constraint order, same batching, same relation balance; the fixtures'
byte-identity is the confirming measurement (it covers the fixture kernel's
chips; the inspection covers the rest).

**2b. Pads cannot unbalance a relation.** Table repeats add no new entry,
and the prover already controls every multiplicity cell; two rows of `v`
with `m_1, m_2` equal one row with `m_1 + m_2`. Code and jump-table pads are
*constrained* to claim nothing: `(1 − is_instruction)·mult = 0`
(`chips/program_memory.rs:211-213`) and `(1 − is_entry)·mult = 0`
(`chips/jump_table.rs:108`); boundary pads are `is_real`-gated. The only
thing that makes a padded table's content trustworthy is its preprocessed
root, which brings M1.

**2c. `bound_sums` is checked correctly.** After `Σ claimed = 0`
(`final-verifier/src/masked.rs:386-404`), from the proof's own states with
the drawn relations, positions from `active`; a missing boundary chip makes
`boundary_positions_in_mask` `None` and the proof is refused. The 34 tampers
include each state field and a public sum. One gap: no *forced* proof with a
non-zero blind on a public-sum chip (the honest prover zeroes them; a prover
that blinds one must be refused by `bound_sums`). Worth one case.

**2d. R1 is complete on every class.** Classes are keyed by
`(level, quotient lift, offsets, h)` (`rank.rs:87-141`), blinding columns are
in the main tree under their chip's `h`, salts and `R` are uniform and never
sampled, the preprocessed tree is public; the gates assert `stats.classes`
equals an independently computed count (11), and `revealed ≤ h/2`.

**2e. What the verifier does *not* pin (hand-offs, not defects in 7.14).**
`verify_segment` (`recair/src/masked/segment.rs:166-199`) takes `active`,
`log_sizes` and the states from the proof and lays the set out from them;
only the format number, config, channel and masking pins are the verifier's.
And `active` is **not mixed into the transcript** (`mix_statement_header`
mixes format, config, log sizes; the unmasked prover does the same, so this
is pre-existing in format 35). The statement is bound through the trees
(different chips → different widths/constraints), so the practical loss is a
weak-Fiat–Shamir gap of at most `log2(#admissible masks)` bits in a contrived
adaptive setting. Pin J's program id (format, mask, log sizes, preprocessed
roots) is the right fix: 7.15 should mix it into the header and verify
against it.

## 3. The gates

Sufficient for the step. Soundness: acceptance at three sizes on both
channels, the device floors (heavy), R1 over every class in one attempt,
three broken-constraint positions, the unbalanced relation with and without
the blinding cancelling it, 34 tampers without a panic, another circuit's
root refused. Leakage: slice 6's seven, honestly adapted. The deviation is
acceptable and correctly reasoned: the node shape publishes the whole
boundary, so under equal public values a run has one witness; the φ9 split
is the same device 6.6 used for the program id, and the device shape
(arguments through the witness input, io-hash only) is where equal public
values admit two witnesses, which 7.15's gates must cover (M3).

Gaps, all small: (i) **no positive case with the honest preprocessed root**:
`support::verify` passes `circuit: None` (`support.rs:125-127`), so the
pinned-root acceptance path is never exercised, only its refusal (M1);
(ii) no "a log size smaller" tamper (likely refused by the tree widths, but
untested); (iii) the forced blind-on-a-public-chip case (2c);
(iv) `the_shape_is_the_format_and_the_coins` overstates (1d).

## 5. Performance: where the ×2.5–2.7 comes from, and what is safe to cut

**Reading the spike's cells** (`spikes/s7-device/runs/x86-real.jsonl`): lean
2:43 plain 34.9M cells → masked 72.8M; lean 1:86 → 76.8M. The masked count is
the column doubling (every column at `2N`) almost exactly: **the floor pads
cost ~4% of cells on this statement**, not the doubling. 7.0a's proxy already
paid the doubled columns *and* the composition two levels up ("commits every
chip one log size up, so the composition two up"), and measured ×1.8–2.1. So
the **proxy→real gap, ×1.34–1.51, is implementation overhead**, not the
masking's mathematics (R's 8 columns, salts and blinding are ~2% of cells).
That gap is the first and safest lever.

Options, in order of expected yield; all keep R1 per transcript and the
format's security; "safe" means the transcript bytes of a seeded run are
unchanged unless stated.

1. **The overhead (×1.3–1.5 → toward ×1.05), behaviour-preserving.**
   Profile `prove_segment` once; the candidates visible in the code:
   - `MaskLayout::new` per `(log_size, h)` class **on every prove**
     (`recair/src/masked/layout.rs:36-116`: an extend, an FFT, a `2N·log N`
     `coset_vanishing` loop and an interpolation per class; 11 classes up to
     `2^16`): structural data, cache it (`OnceLock` by class, or derive at
     install). Est. 20–50 ms x86, ×3.5 on the phone.
   - The trace kept *and cloned per chip per attempt*
     (`masked/trace.rs:126`, `trace.clone()`, because
     `generate_interaction_trace` consumes it), plus `to_circle_evaluation`
     copies then `interpolate_columns` in `prove_masked_checked`
     (`mod.rs:393-404`): borrow the trace, interpolate from it directly, and
     drop the coefficient copy after masking (recompute on the rare R1
     retry). This is the memory lever the record names: real and safe;
     expected peak from ×2.3–2.5 back to about the proxy's ×1.9–2.0.
   - Three component-set constructions per prove (`floors`, `set_layout`,
     `masked_set`), each running an `InfoEvaluator` over every chip
     (`fractions`, `masked/mod.rs:67-74`); build once per shape.
   - `mask_columns`' scatter (`layout.rs:122-141`: `2N` zeros then `N`
     indexed writes per column, ~2,000 columns): write `f` contiguously if
     the extension's coefficient map is a fixed permutation (it is: it is the
     `lower` table), or vectorize.
   - `witness_digest` (BLAKE2b over every main cell, parallel per column,
     `rng.rs:91-130`): ~140 MB on the auth statement, ~35 ms x86 / ~120 ms
     phone; BLAKE3 (already an optional dependency) or a tree over column
     digests cuts it 3–5×. Changes the mask seed derivation, not the proof's
     verification; re-pin the seeded-reproduction test.
   - R1 (`rank_m31`, `rank.rs:226-251`): `O(rows²·h)` per class, 18–24 ms at
     43 queries, 71–73 ms at 86 queries on x86, so **60–250 ms on the
     phone**: 5–15% of the budget. Halving `h` (option 2) halves it.
2. **A tighter `h`: drop the ×2 margin** in `mask_rows` (`mod.rs:139`,
   `revealed·2 → next_pow2`): `h` 512→256 at 43 queries, 1,024→512 at 86;
   floors `2^10`/`2^11`. The guarantee is R1 per transcript, not the margin;
   the functionals are evaluations of a degree-`<h` space at distinct points
   off the trace coset, so the retry rate should stay ~0 (measure it: a retry
   costs a full re-prove). Yield: pads halve (~2–4% here, more on lean
   kernels), R1 ~2× cheaper, half the random draws. Safe; changes proof bytes
   (a new format pin, fine before 43 is registered).
3. **The floor at `2h` rows, not `4h`.** `MaskLayout::new` refuses
   `h > N/4` "so the constraints stay inside the composition's bound"; but a
   cubic constraint over columns of degree `< N + h` has quotient degree
   `2N + 3h ≤ 3.5N < 4N` for `h ≤ N/2`, so one doubling of slack exists. Relax
   the check, re-run the degree audit and the "lifts no constraint past the
   blowup" test, and amend §8. Yield: pads halve again (~2–3% here). Needs a
   two-line proof note; otherwise safe.
4. **The cubic census, the structural lever.** Paired logup is inherently
   cubic (`col·d_1·d_2 − (n_1 d_2 + n_2 d_1)`); `Single` is quadratic. If the
   759 cubic constraints are the `Pairs` chips' batch constraints and no gate
   is cubic (the audit tool prints per-constraint degrees per chip; an hour),
   then a **masked-only `Single` batching for every chip** (the masked eval
   already chooses its own `Logup::batches`; the unmasked path and format 35
   are untouched) makes the masked AIR quadratic, and a quadratic constraint
   over `N + h`-degree columns has quotient `N + 2h ≤ 2N` for `h ≤ N/2`: the
   segment set's `MASKED_LOG_CONSTRAINT_EXPANSION` drops to 1, so the
   composition polynomial, its tree and FRI's first layer halve (this is the
   proxy's "composition two up" becoming one up). Cost: the `Pairs` chips'
   interaction columns double (the CPU's most). Expected net −15–25% of
   masked time after option 1, i.e. toward ×1.8–2.0 overall; memory down
   too. Soundness-neutral (same relations, finer batching); the interaction
   generator (`LogupTraceBuilder::finalize`, pairs centrally) needs a
   `Single` mode on the masked path. If genuine cubic gates exist, the
   expansion stays 2 unless they are rewritten (an AIR change, out of scope).
5. **Per-column `h`** (`|O| = 1` main columns at `h/2`): `mask_columns` already
   takes a per-column `h`; yield is R1 and draws only, no rows. Minor; do
   after 2.
6. **Blowup 2** is already the plan's lever (−20% measured here too);
   **fewer queries at more PoW** reduces `revealed`, hence `h`, R1 and bytes,
   but the PoW's tail is the verifier's and S29's concern.

A caution for 7.16's measurements: pin J's witness-independent trace pads
every chip to the *program id's* sizes, which may sit above the natural
sizes measured here; the floor and lean cells are lower bounds for the
device shape until 7.15 fixes the sizes.

## Findings by severity

**Medium**
- **M1. `verify_segment`'s `circuit: Option<Hash>` and the gates' `None`.**
  `recair/src/masked/segment.rs:166-199`; `tests/segment/support.rs:125-127`.
  Scenario: a caller passes `None` (as every acceptance gate does) and the
  prover chooses the program memory, jump table and *every lookup table*
  (`table_log_size` pads them; nothing but the root pins their content), so
  range checks and the code are unverified. No production path exists yet,
  but 7.15 is about to become one. Fix: make the root mandatory (the device
  program id carries it anyway), carry the preprocessed root in
  `SegmentProof` or compute it from the program, and add the positive gate
  (honest root accepted; the same root for two runs and two kernels of one
  program; another program's refused as `Error::Circuit`).
- **M2. 7.15's prerequisites the code does not yet state.** `bound_sums` and
  `public_sums` require all four boundary chips (`masked/mod.rs:266-275,
  328-339`); pin J's device shape drops MEMORY_ROOT_BOUNDARY and takes the
  io-hash in φ9..12, so both must be generalized (the device's public sums
  are the register boundary/closing and the program boundary; the output
  through φ9..12). And `prove_segment` takes no pinned shape (1d). Fix now:
  a sentence in `masked/mod.rs`'s module doc and the record's hand-off; the
  code in 7.15.
- **M3. The record's zero-knowledge claims need two qualifications**
  (`docs/design/slice-7-progress.md`, the 7.14 entry): `log_sizes` and
  `active` are witness-dependent until pin J's pinned shape (1d), and "one
  shape for two kernels" is by construction of the kernels, not a property
  of the prover; the deviation paragraph should say the device shape's gates
  (two witnesses under equal public values) are 7.15's. Also note the
  transcript does not mix `active` (2e), to be closed by mixing the program
  id.

**Low**
- **L1.** `window_kernel.rs:631` stale doc comment; the repeat assertion no
  longer distinguishes a repeat from a fresh accept (§6).
- **L2.** `MaskedSegmentTrace::main_columns_mut` is an ungated forging seam in
  production builds (`masked/trace.rs:94-98`); gate it or fold it into the
  `seeded-masks` path.
- **L3.** Gate gaps: a forced non-zero blind on a public-sum chip; "a log
  size smaller"; the honest-root positive case (M1).
- **L4.** `Logup::batches(0, 1)` yields one batch for a chip with no
  fractions while `blinded` returns its (empty) columns unchanged
  (`trace.rs:148`): no segment chip has zero fractions, but the two sides
  would disagree silently; assert `n > 0` or handle both consistently.

**Notes (no action)**
- The blinding adds no degree: `deg(b·Πd) = deg(ΔS·Πd)`; the equal histogram
  is exact (1a).
- The weak-FS gap on `active` is pre-existing in format 35 and negligible in
  practice (2e).
- Memory ×2.3–2.5: the kept trace plus its clones and the coefficient copy;
  the fix is a refactor (5.1).

## Verdict

**Fix-then-merge.** Fold M1 (mandatory root + positive gate), M2 and M3 (doc
and record), L1; L2–L4 at will. The masking is sound and hiding on the
segment chips as built; the cost is where the record says it is, and most of
the ×1.3–1.5 above the proxy is reclaimable without touching the
mathematics. The structural lever for pin J's budget is the cubic census
(5.4): if the 759 cubic constraints are the paired logup's, a masked-only
`Single` batching returns the composition to the plain size.
