§7.11 and pin I, `identity-and-authority.md` §5–§7, `node-api.md` §3 and
§6, `node/SECURITY.md`, the 7.11 record and the 7.9, 7.10 and 7.10b

`credentialed` tests (5), the final verifier's `credential` tests (3), the

were re-run and pass. The served suite (three nodes, real proofs), the

**Verdict: fix-then-merge.** The authority core is right: nothing forges an
origin, a proof authorizes one call in one place once, `once` is written on
`Ok` alone and in the same apply, the binding covers every field at the
pinned widths, the verifier is `verify` by kind. Two fixes before the merge
(M1, M2), one record and doc amendment (M3), the rest hand-offs. The k = 22
miss is a property of the setup and of M1 together, not of the verifier
(§4), and S44's wording should change (Q1).

## 1. Authority and privacy

**1a. No origin is forged.** `Anonymous` and `Owner` are made in one place
at ingress, `CredentialPublic::origin` (`agent/wire/src/ingress.rs`), from
public words the proof verified; every other principal a node logs is
constructed from a verified session or a peer's identity
(`node/host/src/api/mod.rs:217`, `node/host/src/calls.rs:299`,
`agent/default/src/refine/inputs.rs:82`, nested calls
`Principal::Instance`). A `Grant` naming a credential's origin is
`Malformed` at the API (`api/operator.rs:73-78`) and `Forbidden` at apply
(`refine/admin.rs:78-82`); a guard admits its kind alone and a method
without one refuses every credential's origin (`refine/invocation.rs:179-187`),
on the one path every command, query, nested call and credentialed input
takes. A guard sits only on a command, so no workflow or query admits one
(`schema.rs:guard_fits`). The leader takes a forwarded
`CredentialedInput` on a replica's word (`calls.rs:635-682`,
`peer::check`), which is the trust-boundary rule and is stated in
`node/SECURITY.md`.

**1b. The binding is exact.** `Bound::digest`
(`agent/wire/src/credentials.rs:674-694`) hashes space (32), agent,
instance and method (4 LE each), program (32), kind (1), the `u32` LE
length then the arguments, nonce (16), `expires_ms` (8 LE), under
`"vos/cred/binding/v1"`; `binding` is `C(T(binding), W(b))` (`:696`).
Ingress recomputes it from the request's own fields and the agent the
frame addresses (`node/host/src/credentialed.rs:195-209`), after
`present(agent)` (`:115`), so another space, agent, instance, method,
argument byte, nonce or expiry fails `Binding`. The program is the
circuit's by kind (`verifier::program`), so 7.12's format choice moves it
with the format. The wire test pins the hash and that each field moves it.

**1c. The format and the verifier.** Ingress admits
`verifier::FORMAT` alone (`credentialed.rs:39`), whose test pins it to the
registry's `CREDENTIAL_PROOF` (`final-verifier/src/credential.rs:283-320`);
no literal 42 outside the verifier. It calls `verify_bytes` → `verify`
(`credential.rs:197-220`), which checks the word count, the kind word and
every word below p, then `verify_with` under the kind's pinned
preprocessed root; `verify_with` is `pub` for the tests and the joint
circuit's measurement only, and the fuzz target is `verify_bytes`
(`fuzz_targets/credential_verify_bytes.rs`). The 7.10 hand-off (L3) is
closed, and the PoW timing was measured (0.66 ms), not assumed.

**1d. Replay, forwarding, `once`.** A binding is checked at ingress on
applied state (`credentialed.rs:210-212`) and at apply under the batch's
overlay (`refine/credentialed.rs:43-45`, `base.holds`), so a twin in one
batch and a copy at another replica each run once; `b` is recorded
whatever the method does (`:100`), the `once` row only on `Outcome::Done`
in the same step (`:101-106`). The sweep forgets `b` at `expires_ms + 60 s`
by batch time; a replay accepted by an ingress whose wall clock lags the
batch time by more than a minute is still refused at apply by step 1
(`expires_ms < t`), so the sweep cannot open a window. A follower that
forwarded a call and hears `Replayed` answers only once it holds the
binding (`calls.rs:577-586, :591-622`): the 7.11 Opus M2, right. Two
proofs of one nullifier in one scope: the first's `once` write sits in the
overlay before the second's check. The race tests
(`tests/served/credentialed.rs:566-620`) hold the leader's refines and
send the copies to the leader and a follower: the right test.

**1e. Scopes and linkability.** `scope` hashes space, agent (4 LE),
instance (4 LE) and the guard's name under `"vos/cred/scope/v1"`
(`credentials.rs:636-647`); ingress derives it from the guard, never from
the request (`credentialed.rs:189-194`), and apply checks it again against
the guard before running (`refine/credentialed.rs:79-99`). A nullifier is
`C(C(T(nf), s), scope)`, so one member's pseudonyms in two scopes, two
agents or two spaces sharing a handle are unlinkable by the log; tested
at the wire and served. The `Anonymous` origin carries scope and
nullifier, which is what the log must carry for `once`. The log carries
`InputRef::Credentialed { binding }` and the effects, never the proof,
nonce or format (`batch.rs:CredentialedInput`); the served test asserts
no entry holds a proof's bytes.

**1f. What is not an issue, said once.** The apply-time scope re-check is
not in §5's rule; it costs a BLAKE2b and a Poseidon2 per auth call (on
JAM in software) and defends against a node logging a scope not the
guard's, which a node could do only as it could log any origin. Harmless;
it is the measured 165k on JAM in part. Keep or drop; if kept, §5's rule
should list it.

## 2. Check order and denial of service

The order is pin I's: frame size from the header (`vos-api`'s limit
1,642,496; HTTP 413 from the head, `node/http/src/server.rs:384-392`),
decode, `present` (`NoAgent`/`Elsewhere`), then `check`
(`credentialed.rs:167-247`): `NoSpace`, `Format`, `Size` (proof against
its format's cap, arguments against `cap::REQUEST`), `Expiry` by the
node's clock, `Method`, `Kind`, `Scope`, `Binding`, `Replayed`, `Once`,
`RootUnknown`/`RootStale` by the replica's latest batch time
(`fresh`, `:277-284`); then words at or above p refused `Proof` without a
slot (cheaper first; the record says so); then the slot (`Verifying`,
`:133-136`, `Busy` past twice the executors) and the verification on an
executor, the place released with the job however it ends. Every
pre-slot check is a few reads and two hashes; the dearest is decoding the
installed record per request (Opus L4, noted, fine for now).
`RootUnknown` is 503 and retryable (7.9's L2 closed).

**The one gap, M1: a credentialed call's body and its place live through
the apply wait.** `respond` decodes `(agent, call)` from `&body` and keeps
`body` alive until the answer (`api/mod.rs:172-177`, the parameter of
`respond`); the socket's large place is moved into the request's task and
dropped after the answer is written (`api/socket.rs:171-177`), QUIC's and
HTTP's the same way. Since every credential proof (~200 KB) is past a
call's limit, every credentialed call takes one of 64 places
(`api/mod.rs:93, :127`; `server.rs:44-46`) for its verification *and* its
apply wait, including a forward's up to 30 s (`FORWARDING`) and the
`recorded` wait. So (i) the node's credentialed concurrency is 64 across
socket and QUIC, a throughput cap of 64 / latency: 12,800 a second at
5 ms, 2,100 at 30 ms, 700 at 92 ms, which is the k = 22 row's regime
(§4); (ii) a member with 64 valid proofs sent to a follower whose leader
is slow or partitioned holds every place for 30 s, the socket's reader
then waits on `acquire_arc` (`socket.rs:207`), head-of-line for that
connection's other frames, and QUIC stops new credentialed streams: a
cheap starvation of the node's credentialed ingress by one member. Fix:
drop `body` once decoded (take it by value in the `Credentialed` arm), and
release the place when the verification ends (the proof is gone by then;
the `CredentialedInput` is at most 64 KiB of arguments), e.g. by handing
the place to `Inner::credentialed` and dropping it with `proof`, or by
counting bytes rather than bodies. Then re-run k = 22.

The verification budget itself is right: `Verifying::take` is a CAS, the
place goes with the executor's job (Opus L2), no bucket per scope or
method. The budget bounds verifications in flight, not the executor queue
behind them; at k = 22 the queue time is unmeasured (§4).

## 3. The apply rule, the rows and replication

**3a. The rule is §5's, in order** (`refine/credentialed.rs:40-107`):
expired, `Replayed`, `StaleRoot` (a root the ring lacks or an agent with
no ring is `StaleRoot` at apply, as the rule says: no retry at apply),
`Once`, then the binding row and its due row, the method as the origin,
the `once` row on `Ok`. A refused step writes nothing (`Step::Refused`).
A ring the meter cannot pay fails the call consumed and unrecorded
(Opus L5, documented): deterministic per batch, since the meter is the
batch's. Every read is of replicated state through `Base`, so VOS
replicas and a JAM refine agree; the gas differs by host (80k VOS, 165k
JAM) but outcomes depend on gas only where the budget binds (3c).

**3b. The sweep.** `Due::Binding` (kind 2, a 32-byte id, keys prefix-free
by the kind byte, `keys.rs:due`), listed within `SweepBudget` (`keys.rs:
922-958`): 32 sessions' and workflows' rows and 64 bindings' beside them,
96 keys looked at, on the leader (`leader/sweep.rs:103-124`) and again in
the refine (`inputs.rs:sweep`), so a leader listing more is capped;
`DECODE_DUE` (2,000) per listed row in `batch_start`; `Step::Swept {
bindings }` deletes both rows in accumulate (`apply.rs:forget`). Priced:
a full sweep with 64 bindings under `SWEEP_RESERVE`, a sweep of its most
rows under `batch_start` (`pricing.rs`). Right. Two notes: the drain is
64 bindings a sweep and a sweep a batch while rows are due, so sustained
credentialed load above 64 × the batch rate grows the binding set and,
once more than 96 bindings are due before a session's row, occludes
sessions in the listing window until it drains (L4: state the drain
rate, or size `SWEEP_BINDINGS` to the gate); `first_due` with `limit = 1`
looks at up to 65 keys for one row (L3).

**3c. M2: the ring is priced by `RECORD_BYTE` per auth call, on a ring of
two.** `ring()` charges `RECORD_BYTE × len` of the ring's row before
decoding it (`refine/credentialed.rs:149`), on every credentialed input,
with no cache; `installed()` pays a record once a refine and keeps it
(`base.rs:271-292`). A full ring is 64 × 40 + 2 ≈ 2,562 bytes, so 68 ×
2,562 ≈ 174k gas per auth call, against the 5.6k the own-work test paid
on its ring of two entries (`found` and one `enroll`,
`tests/agent/credentialed.rs:601-626`). The charge is accounting, drawn
from the batch's headroom above the step's reserve (`Meter::take`,
`base.rs:71-82`), so it is not a failure in practice: a batch of auth
calls on a busy space (any space past 64 tree-changing batches) pays
~174k more a call than measured, on both hosts, which is a third to a
half of a step's budget and that many fewer calls a batch; and the
record's "within a fifth to spare" holds only on a young ring. Fix: pay
and decode the ring once a refine as installed records are (a cache on
`Base`), or price it per entry by a constant of its own; re-measure the
own work at a ring of 64 (`enroll_many` fills it in one call) and record
that number. Deterministic either way.

**3d. The install rules** (`admin.rs:credentials`): one actor, its
instance not replaceable by a non-actor, an auth guard needs the actor,
each `Failure::Credentials`; tested. The schema section's trailer is
decoded only past the methods and an empty trailer is refused
(`schema.rs:section, :from_ro_data`), so an unguarded program's section
is byte for byte the old one: the right way to keep `WINDOW_PROGRAM` and
`PREDICATE_PROGRAM` still.

## 4. The rate gate (S44)

The table shows the bound met to k = 16 and missed at k = 22 with the
verification's mean at 8.8 ms against 1.8 ms alone. Three things say the
miss is the setup's and M1's, not the verifier's:

- **The budget per thread is 10 ms.** `100·k` calls a second on `k`
  executors is 10 ms of executor time a call, verification and apply
  together. At k = 16 the mean verification is already 5.65 ms (SMT
  siblings and E-cores at work: the host is 6 P-cores with SMT, 8 E-cores,
  2 LP E-cores), at k = 22 8.8 ms, leaving ~1 ms for the apply and the
  runtime. Any host whose logical threads verify in more than ~7 ms under
  full load fails the gate as worded, whatever the node does.
- **Little's law.** 1,738 a second at a p50 of 92 ms is ~160 calls in
  flight, but the bench has 96 senders (`benches/credentialed.rs:43`)
  and, after the Opus M1 fold, the node holds 64 credentialed bodies
  (§2): 64 places / 1,738 a second is 37 ms of holding time a call, so
  the measured rate at k = 22 is plausibly the places' cap, with the
  latency from "due" (`:229-234`) counting the wait for a sender and a
  place. At k = 16 ~18 were in flight, under both bounds.
- **The host.** The generator (96 threads), the node's runtime and the
  applies share the 22 threads with the verifications; the load was 4 to
  20 from other agents' runs; the k = 22 run lasted 1.4 s.

So the verifier is within pin I (1.87 / 2.11 ms, PoW refusal 0.66 ms),
the node's executors are within the bound where each is a core, and the
gate's statement conflates the host's logical threads with verifying
cores. Recommendation: fix M1 and re-run k = 22 here (the cap lifted,
the number should rise toward the host's compute); then measure the gate
as stated under Q1 on compumax, the node pinned to its physical cores
(`taskset`, `executors` = that count, no SMT siblings), the generator on
the laptop over QUIC (or on cores apart), runs of at least 10 s, the
machine otherwise idle; record `N` as physical and logical. Add to the
stats the executor queue wait of a verification (the time from
`Verifying::take` to the closure's start), so the bench says whether a
miss is verification or queueing, and print the calls in flight.

## 5. The re-pins and the reserves

**`BALANCE_PROGRAM`** moved because the balance kernel formats a
`Failure`, which gained `Credentials` (15); no fixture holds it, the
`constants::` test checks it, and `WINDOW_PROGRAM`, `PREDICATE_PROGRAM`
and the derive pins are unchanged by the guarded trailer. This is the ABI
change pin I schedules before 7.8b, so no pinned blob moves twice; safe.

**The reserves** (`DECODE_INPUT` 6,000 → 7,000, `INSTALL_RESERVE` 9M →
10M, `RECORD_BYTE` 64 → 68) follow the 65-byte principal and the guard
field and are re-measured by the pricing tests; raising a reserve can
only defer an input to a later batch, never fail one, and
`min_refine_gas` grows with them. Safe. `RECORD_BYTE` also prices the
ring (M2), where it is the wrong unit.

**The sizes:** `MAX_CREDENTIAL_PROOF` 1.5 MiB, the kind's limit
1,642,496 = 1,572,864 + 65,536 + 4,096, HTTP 4 less, `ADMITTED` caps 42
at 512 KiB: as pinned, tested at the API and at ingress.

## 6. Code quality

Where it matters it is good: `credentialed.rs` is one check function in
pin order with one refusal type, `Forwardable` folds the forwarding
without a second copy of `forward`, `Pending::Credentialed` reuses the
queue and the settle, the schema trailer is small and exact. Small
things: the ring's row key is derived twice, at ingress
(`credentialed.rs:287-301`) and in the refine
(`refine/credentialed.rs:135-153`), from `tables::RING` and `RING_KEY`;
a `credentials::ring_key(actor)` in the wire would hold it once (L2). In
`check`, the kind match and the scope `if let` could be one match on
`(&guard, &call.public)` (nit). `node-api.md:170-173` still says a
credentialed body "is not large (only an install's is)", which the M1
fold made untrue (L5). The 7.10b hand-off on the operator nonce margin
(forget a nonce past `expires + HORIZON`) was not taken and the record
does not say so (L6); bindings have their margin (`BINDING_KEPT_MS`) and
the apply-time expiry, so it is the operator path's alone.

## Findings by severity

**Medium**
- **M1. Credentialed bodies and their places are held through the apply
  wait.** `node/host/src/api/mod.rs:172-177` (`body` alive until the
  answer), `api/socket.rs:171-177, :207`, `api/quic.rs:239`,
  `node/http/src/server.rs:384-417`; 64 places (`api/mod.rs:93, :127`,
  `server.rs:46`). Caps credentialed concurrency at 64 across socket and
  QUIC, so throughput at 64 / latency, and lets one member with 64 valid
  proofs to a follower of a slow leader hold every place for 30 s. Fix:
  drop the body after decode; release the place with the proof, after the
  verification; re-run k = 22. Add a test that a credentialed call past
  its verification holds no place.
- **M2. The ring is priced per call by `RECORD_BYTE`, measured on a ring
  of two.** `agent/default/src/refine/credentialed.rs:149`;
  `tests/agent/credentialed.rs:601-626`. A full ring costs ~174k a call
  from the batch's headroom, unmeasured. Fix: cache the ring per refine as
  `Base::installed` does, or a per-entry price; re-measure the own work
  at 64 roots and record it.
- **M3. The record and the docs on the gate and the bodies.** The 7.11
  entry's k = 22 paragraph should add the places' cap (M1) and the 10 ms
  per-thread budget as the reasons, beside the host; `node-api.md:170-173`
  should say credentialed bodies are large and take places of their own
  (L5); `node/SECURITY.md`'s denial-of-service list should state the
  concurrency the places imply once M1 fixes where they are released.

**Low**
- **L1.** `calls.rs:591-622`: a follower lagging past a binding's sweep
  (`expires + 60 s` by batch time) answers a forwarded `Replayed` as
  `TimedOut`. Rare; return `Replayed` once `applied ≥ at` of the leader's
  answer instead, or say so in the doc comment.
- **L2.** The ring's row key derived twice (§6); one wire helper.
- **L3.** `leader/sweep.rs:94-99`: `first_due` lists up to 65 keys for one
  row through `SweepBudget::rows`; pass a budget of one row.
- **L4.** The binding set's drain is 64 a sweep, a sweep a batch: state the
  rate against the gate's load in `defaults.rs` or the record, or size
  `SWEEP_BINDINGS` to it; note the occlusion of sessions past 96 due
  bindings.
- **L5.** `node-api.md:170-173` stale (M3).
- **L6.** The 7.10b operator-nonce margin hand-off not taken; say so in the
  record or carry it to 7.13.
- **L7.** The bench: print calls in flight and the executor queue wait;
  say in its doc that `SENDERS` bounds the generator at 96 / latency.
- **L8.** `Busy` and the credentialed places have no end-to-end test (the
  record says why for `Busy`); a `large_places` unit test by kind and body
  size is cheap.

**Notes (no action)**
- The apply-time scope re-check is beyond §5's rule and costs a
  Poseidon2 on JAM (1f); keep and list it, or drop it.
- `Proof` for a word at or above p precedes `Busy`: cheaper first, said in
  the record; fine.
- Two methods of one instance sharing a guard name share a scope and,
  with `once`, one admission set: by construction of the scope; worth a
  sentence in the SDK docs when 7.13 writes them.
- `fresh` with no batch time yet judges at 0: before any batch only.

## Verdict

**Fix-then-merge.** M1 (free the body and the place after the
verification; re-run k = 22), M2 (the ring paid once a refine, re-measured
at 64 roots), M3 (the record and the two docs); L1–L8 at will. The
authority core, the check order, the apply rule and the replicated rows
are as pinned and tested where it counts; the re-pins and the reserves are
safe. The gate's k = 22 is the host's threads and the places' cap, not the
verifier's, and should be measured as Q1 decides.
