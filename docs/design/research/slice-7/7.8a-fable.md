## 1. Item 13, the witness digest (`40614908`)

**Sound.** The digest is the prover's alone: it enters only the ChaCha seed
`BLAKE2b-256(SEED_DOMAIN ‖ entropy ‖ witness ‖ attempt)`
(`pvm/proof/recair/src/masked/rng.rs:49-69`), never the proof, so there is
no prover/verifier agreement to keep; the verifier sees nothing of it.

**Binding.** The new word list is a superset of the old, in a
self-delimiting form:

- old: `format.number, pow_bits, n_queries, log_blowup, log_last_layer,
  fold_step, lifting (1 + l or 0), batching, interaction_pow, channel,
  salt_columns, fri_mask_columns, log_mask_rows, log_sizes.len(),
  log_sizes…`;
- new: `header.len(), header…, batching, interaction_pow, channel,
  salt_columns, fri_mask_columns, log_mask_rows`, where `header` is
  `statement_header_words` (`pvm/proof/src/proof/transcript.rs:30-54`):
  the two domain words, `format_version, pow_bits, n_queries, log_blowup,
  log_last_layer, fold_step`, lifting as `[0]` or `[1, l]`, then
  `log_sizes…`.

Every old word is still hashed. The two variable-length pieces inside the
header (lifting, 1 or 2 words; `log_sizes`, `n` words) are unambiguous
given `header.len()`: the word after `fold_step` is `0` or `1` and fixes
the lifting's width, so `log_sizes.len()` is determined; the explicit
count the old list carried is redundant and its removal loses nothing.
Public values and the main columns follow as before. R4's claim in
`proving.md` §5 ("binding the statement and the main columns") and the
module doc (`rng.rs:1-13`) remain true; the function's doc (`rng.rs:81-90`)
was rewritten to match the code.

**Leakage.** The digest reaches a carrier only through BLAKE2b into the
seed; the bytes themselves are `drawn::note`d
(`pvm/proof/recair/src/masked/mod.rs:336`) and the carrier audit needles
every `Drawn` as `Kind::WrapRandomness`
(`node/checker/src/carriers/needles.rs:300-306`). The record's `test-proof`
run reports the audit clean over 451 such needles, so the new digest's
bytes were searched for. The leakage gates (`recair/tests/circuit/leakage.rs:124-141`)
prove through `prove_masked_on` with `MaskRng::Seeded(seed)`, which
computes `witness_digest` on every proof, so they ran on the new digest at
medium N (the record: 562 passed, 3,223 s). One witness, many seeds, is
the right shape for that gate; the digest is constant across it, as it
should be.

**L3 (Low, test).** `a_replayed_state_draws_other_masks_for_another_witness`
(`rng.rs:148-191`) varies the witness, the channel and the public values,
but not what item 13 changed: a `log_sizes` or a `format.number` or a
masking parameter. Scenario: a later edit drops `header` from the hash by
mistake (or hashes it twice in a way that cancels nothing, which is
harmless); the test still passes. Fix: add two digests to the four, one
at `log_sizes: &[5]` and one under a format with another `number` or
`masking.log_mask_rows`, and assert they differ.

## 3. `array::from_fn` over `as_chunks` (`a2e4648c`)

Correct as written: `hash.as_chunks::<8>()` on a `[u8; 32]` yields exactly
four `[u8; 8]` with an empty remainder, so `from_fn(|k| u64::from_le_bytes(chunks[k]))`
reads the same words the slice-and-`try_into` closure did, and the
measurement in the record shows why the slicing form was refused (the
sixteen-row read +8.0%, past the 10% budget).

**Is relying on byte identity robust?** It is pinned where it matters and
not elsewhere:

- what is pinned: `WINDOW_PROGRAM`, `BALANCE_PROGRAM`, `PREDICATE_PROGRAM`
  recomputed from the test actors' ELFs in
  `pvm/proof/tests/kernel/constants.rs:41` (`test-proof`, `justfile:91`,
  under both prover feature sets) and on aarch64 by `repro-aarch64`
  (`justfile:134-136`); the toolchain is pinned
  (`rust-toolchain.toml`, `nightly-2025-07-14`). A toolchain bump that
  changed the codegen would fail `constants::` and be a re-pin on its own
  terms, which is the same exposure the old closure had.
- what is not: the other test actors, the agent image and the
  representative, which the record says are byte-identical by a local
  comparison, have no committed hash until 7.8b's `blobs.lock` (L2). That
  is the plan's order and the right one; nothing here should pin them
  early.

So the trick is robust in the sense that matters: if it ever stops being
byte-identical, the pins say so, and the fallback is a re-pin, not a
soundness question.

## 6. Code quality

Good throughout: each commit does one thing, the messages say what moved
and what did not, the doc comments are timeless. Two nits, neither
blocking:

- **L4 (Low, totality).** `fri_fold_rows(layers) = 5 * layers + (layers - 2)`
  and `domain_point_rows(log_size) = 2 + 4 * (log_size - 2) + 1`
  (`pvm/compiler/src/leaf.rs:415-425`) underflow at `layers < 2` and
  `log_size < 2`, where the old `6F − 2` was total down to `F = 0` (the old
  `4L − 5` underflowed at `L = 1` too). Unreachable in practice: the
  smallest chip log size is 4 and `lifting = max_log + LOG_BLOWUP`. But
  the functions now read as if they were general. Fix: either write them
  as the closed forms with the derivation in the doc (`6 * layers - 2`
  "five a layer plus the doubling past the second") or add a
  `debug_assert!(layers >= 2)` naming the precondition.
- `Mode::of` as `ALL.into_iter().find(..)` (`guest/mod.rs:54-58`) is fine
  in guest code; a three-arm `match` would be one line shorter and
  const-evaluable, but this is taste.
- `check_composition(&set, layout, ..)` takes `layout` only for
  `air_log_size(layout)`; passing the level in would make the seam's
  inputs explicit. Taste.
