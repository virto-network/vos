### M1. `anchor` checks `held` before `Age`: an aged second root ends the space only while the first is still stored

- **Where:** `pvm/proof/anchor/src/venue.rs:234-252` (`held` at 234,
  `fresh` at 250); the pruning that decides it: `node/venue/src/kreivo/mock/pallet.rs:594-613`
  (`on_idle`, "may stop part way"), `pvm/proof/statement/service/src/accumulate.rs:53,467-496`

- **Scenario:** a space's node signed two roots for one window `w` (the
  pre-7.2 honest fault of `plan3-fable.md` M1: a deposed leader's cut

  it is `WindowNotAfterLast` (`w ≤ Last`); at one whose `on_idle` or
  accumulate has not reached that row, or at the model, it is
  `Evidence`: the space ends. Whether a stale signature is fatal depends
  on prune lag, so the venues do not judge alike by construction, and the
  owner's "a stolen key ends the space" (S11) is widened to "a stale
  signature of a since-superseded root ends it, for a day plus the prune
  lag".
- **Fix:** test freshness before the held root:
  `if !fresh(&space.clock, w, state.now()) { return Err(Age) }` moved
  above line 234 (an aged `Again` becomes `Age`; both settle at the node).
  Add a suite case to `suite.rs::equivocation`: a second root at a window
  held but aged, `Refused(Age)`, the status still `Active`. Regenerate the
  vectors: G4, G5 and G16 should be byte-identical (their equivocations

## Judgements, by the brief

1. **Sound and complete?** Yes for every stated rule; nothing else is
   checked. No wrong statement or anchor is accepted (the proof binds the
   roots, the venue binds each root to its space, window and freshness; a
   repeat is `Seen`). An honest space can be ended by a stale second
   signature within the prune lag (M1) and, until 7.2, by an honest
   double-sign (L4); not stalled: `Blocked` keeps the honest node's
   evidence queued at Kreivo (`Answer::Wait`). The tombstone is not
   bypassable under the same `SpaceId`.
2. **Do the venues and clients call the core with no residual rule logic?**
   The anchor rule, registration, `anchored` and the freshness arithmetic
   live in the core alone (the gate confirms). Residual: the key-listing
   rule at three sites (L2), the refine-first signature on JAM (L1), and
   the clients' sanctioned status and key reads (L5). The gate is
   meaningful as a tripwire and narrow (L3).
3. **Vectors and v2.** G1–G17 and §0 are a faithful spec of what changed;
   G5 carries the tombstone, the deposit release and
   `dispatch_as_sovereign` on an ended space, G15 the ended space under
   `set_status`, G16 the kept tombstone through `on_idle`, G14 and G17 the
   small-order and duplicate keys and `AuthorityExists`. `mutations.json`
   kills at the right codes (none at 7; the venue-id mutants at 0x108). v2
   should also carry L1's order note and M3's known limit.
4. **Did a removal drop a check that still matters?** Rootless handling and
   `venue_bound` guarded the cross-venue replay of a *venue-bound* program's
   statement; with every statement rooted that guard is the space's one
   venue, which the venue cannot enforce (M3). Migration ranges never
   guarded dual registration either. Nothing else was dropped: the `Seen`
   expiry, the served path's checks in refine, the signature and key rules
   all stand.
5. **The statement service.** The trap is not submitter-reachable; the pins
   are bounded and canonical; the split is right (refine stateless,
   accumulate the state), with one ordering consequence (L1).
6. **Quality and simplicity.** Simpler by every count the record gives,
   and the record's deltas match the diff; the code reads well; the
   remaining duplication is L2.
